Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026. Plain English first, details second.

Summary: GradeCalculator does not collect, transmit, or store the marks you enter. All calculations happen locally in your browser.

What we collect

Calculator inputs: The numbers you type (current marks, final total, target) are processed entirely client-side in JavaScript. They are never sent to our servers, never written to a database, and never shared with third parties. Closing the tab discards them.

Contact: If you email yashkharb37@gmail.com or use the contact form (which opens your mail app), we receive your name, email address, and message so we can reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list unless you explicitly ask.

No accounts, no tracking cookies: We don’t require sign-up, we don’t set advertising or analytics cookies, and we don’t build profiles.

What we don’t collect

  • We don’t store your marks or calculation history on a server.
  • We don’t use fingerprinting or cross-site trackers.
  • We don’t sell personal data — we don’t have it to sell.

Local storage

We don’t currently use localStorage or sessionStorage to persist your inputs. If that changes (for example, to remember your last calculation on your device), we’ll update this policy and note it in the UI.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may generate standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and reliability. Those logs are not linked to your calculator inputs and are retained only as long as needed for operational purposes.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) to render Inter and JetBrains Mono. When your browser fetches those fonts, Google may receive your IP address and user agent as part of a normal web request. No calculator data is sent to Google.

We don’t embed analytics, ads, or social widgets that would otherwise track you across sites.

Children’s privacy

The calculator is useful for students of any age, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can use the calculator without providing any personal information.
  • You can block fonts or JavaScript in your browser — the page will still convey information, though the calculator requires JavaScript to compute.
  • You can request deletion of any email correspondence by writing to yashkharb37@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example, analytics or persistent storage), we’ll update this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be noted clearly on the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email yashkharb37@gmail.com.
