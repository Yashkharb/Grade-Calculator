Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026. Plain English first, details second.

Summary: The arithmetic runs locally in your browser and GradeCalculator does not maintain a calculator history. We may collect visitor usage data through Google Analytics. Shared calculation links contain the entered values in their URL, so those values may be visible to recipients and included in normal server or analytics requests when opened.

What we collect

Calculator inputs: The numbers you type (current marks, final total, target) are used by client-side JavaScript to calculate your result. We do not maintain a calculation history in a GradeCalculator database. If you use Copy link, the values are encoded in the URL (e.g. ?earned=72&possible=80&final=100&target=90) so the recipient can restore the same calculation in their browser. Because the URL contains those values, anyone with the link can see them, and opening the link may make the URL available to hosting logs or Google Analytics. Do not put sensitive information in a share link.

Contact: If you email yashkharb37@gmail.com or use the contact form (which opens your mail app), we receive your name, email address, and message so we can reply. We don’t add you to a mailing list unless you explicitly ask.

Analytics: We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor usage. Depending on configuration, it may collect page views, interactions, device and browser information, approximate location, and cookie or similar identifiers. Analytics data is handled by Google under its own privacy terms. We do not use calculator inputs to create an account or maintain a calculation history.

What we don’t collect

  • We don’t maintain your marks or calculation history in a GradeCalculator database.
  • We don’t require an account to use the calculator.
  • We don’t sell personal data.

Share links

The Copy link feature creates a URL that contains your inputs as query parameters. The values are visible in the URL, so anyone you share it with can read them. When someone opens the link, the URL is part of the normal request and may be available to hosting logs and Google Analytics. The calculation itself is restored locally in JavaScript; do not use a share link for sensitive information.

Local storage

We don’t use localStorage or sessionStorage to persist your inputs. The page does not remember form values after you close it. This does not remove values from a share URL, server log, analytics record, or message where you have already shared them.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may generate standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and reliability. A requested URL may include the calculator values from a shared link. Logs are retained by the hosting provider according to its policies and operational needs.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) to render Inter and JetBrains Mono. When your browser fetches those fonts, Google may receive your IP address and user agent as part of a normal web request.

We may also use Google Analytics for visitor measurement. It can collect usage information such as page views, events or interactions, device/browser details, approximate location, and cookie or similar identifiers. If a shared calculation URL is opened, its query-string values may be included in the page URL available to analytics. Review Google’s policies for how Google handles analytics data.

Children’s privacy

The calculator is useful for students of any age, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can use the calculator without creating an account or providing contact information.
  • You can block fonts or JavaScript in your browser — the page will still convey information, though the calculator requires JavaScript to compute.
  • You can manage cookies and analytics permissions through your browser settings and, where available, the site’s consent controls. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can request deletion of any email correspondence by writing to yashkharb37@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, including the analytics provider, analytics settings, or persistent storage, we’ll update this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be noted clearly on the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email yashkharb37@gmail.com.
