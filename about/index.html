About the project — Know your number

A calculator that answers one question well.

GradeCalculator exists for the moment before a final exam when you want a straight answer — not a dashboard. Know your number before you walk in.

What it does — Know your number

Before you walk into the final, know your number. You enter three things: marks you’ve earned so far (e.g. 72), how many marks were possible (e.g. 80), and how many marks the final is worth (e.g. 100). Then you pick your target overall percentage.

The calculator tells you the minimum whole-number score you need on the final to reach that target — plus a what-if slider to explore any score, a target explorer for every achievable grade, your buffer above the minimum, and a shareable link whose values are visible in the URL. It assumes the current marks and the final are weighted equally by their point totals (the most common pattern) and is explicit when that assumption doesn’t apply.

Why it exists

Students often have to reverse-engineer a grade from a syllabus table or a spreadsheet someone else built. That’s slow, error-prone, and stressful right before an exam.

This tool is intentionally small and product-polished:

  • Fast — one form, one answer. Calculator appears on first scroll on mobile.
  • Client-side calculation — the arithmetic runs in your browser. The site may collect visitor usage data through Google Analytics, and share links encode values in the URL you control.
  • Clear — exact vs. minimum shown, buffer explained, weighting limits flagged plainly.
  • Useful beyond the answer — what-if slider, target explorer, and gap visualization turn “is it possible?” into “what is possible?”.

Who it’s for

Any student who knows their current fraction (e.g. 72/80) and the size of the final. It’s useful for high school, college, and university courses where the final is a single exam with a known point total. If your course uses category weightings (e.g. homework 30%, midterm 30%, final 40%), this calculator isn’t the right model — check your syllabus weighting instead.

Clear data boundaries

No account is required. Calculator inputs are used for the calculation; visitor usage may be measured through Google Analytics.

Instant

Type, press Enter, see the answer. No page reloads or spinners.

Accurate

Validated maths with clear rounding — 89.2 becomes 90, not 89.

How the calculation works

Overall % = (earned + finalObtained) ÷ (possible + finalTotal) × 100

We solve for finalObtained:

required = ceil(target/100 × (possible + finalTotal) − earned)

If the result is ≤ 0, you already have enough. If it’s > finalTotal, an A isn’t mathematically possible and we show your maximum overall percentage. Otherwise we show the required marks and the percentage that represents.

See the FAQ for handling of fractional marks, weighting assumptions, and other grade boundaries.

Product language — “Know your number”

We use “Know your number.” and “No guessing. Just the maths.” not as slogans, but as a promise: you’ll see the exact integer you need, why it’s that number, your live position (72/80 = 90%), what happens if you score 80/90/100, and every target still within reach. The first screen should answer Where am I? What do I enter? What will I get? in under three seconds.

No invented story

This is a small, independent educational tool — not a company, not a team, not a startup with a funding round. It was built to be useful, accessible, fast, and honest about what it does and doesn’t do. No AI claims, no invented “smart predictions” — just accurate arithmetic, clear disclosures, great UX, and speed.
