Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Short, accurate answers about the maths, the edge cases, and your privacy.

How is the required final-exam score calculated?

Overall % = (marks you’ve earned + marks on final) ÷ (possible so far + final total) × 100.

We solve for marks on final:

required = ceil(target ÷ 100 × (possible + finalTotal) − earned)

The ceil is the “round up” — you need the smallest whole mark that actually reaches the target.

What does “minimum marks required” mean?

It’s the lowest integer score on the final that still gets you to your target overall. If the exact maths says 89.2, 89 isn’t enough — you need 90.

What happens if I already have enough marks for an A?

You’ll see 0 / final total and a message like “You already have enough marks for an A.” We also show what your overall would be even with 0, so you can see your buffer.

What if an A is mathematically impossible?

We tell you clearly and show your maximum overall percentage even with a perfect final. For example: “Even with 100/100, your maximum would be 86.1%.” Use that to set a realistic target or talk to your instructor.

Why does the calculator round up?

Most exams award whole marks. If you need 89.2, 89 won’t get you there. Rounding up guarantees the result you see is actually sufficient. When the exact requirement is already an integer, no rounding is needed.

Does the calculator assume the current and final marks are weighted equally?

By total points, yes. The final’s weight is its point total relative to the current total. That matches many courses where “80 marks so far + 100 on the final = 180 total.”

If your syllabus instead says “the final is 40% of the grade regardless of points,” use a weighted grade calculator or adjust the inputs to reflect the weighting.

Can I use this calculator for grades other than A?

Yes. Change the target to 60%, 70%, 50% — whatever your course requires. The maths is identical. Presets for 70/80/85/90/95 are built in for quick switching.

What happens if my final exam has a different maximum score?

Enter that number as the final total — 50, 75, 120, whatever. The calculator adapts automatically.

Are my marks stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Marks are never sent to a server, never stored in cookies, and never logged. See the Privacy Policy for the plain-English version.

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes — completely free, no account, no paywall. It’s a single-page calculator, not a subscription product.
