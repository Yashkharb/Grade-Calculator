1. What this site is

GradeCalculator is a free, educational calculator. It helps you estimate the marks you need on a final exam to reach a target overall percentage, using the formula shown on the homepage.

It is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute academic advice.

2. Accuracy and limitations

We work hard to keep the maths correct and to handle edge cases (already achieved, impossible targets, fractional rounding). However:

  • The calculator assumes current marks and the final are weighted equally by point totals. If your course uses category weightings, the result may not match your school’s official calculation.
  • Rounding is to the next whole mark. If your institution allows half-marks or has different rounding rules, verify with your syllabus.
  • We don’t guarantee the calculator is error-free or always available. Use it as a guide and double-check against your official grading information.
