How it works

We combine what you have with what you need.

Your current marks and the final are added together. We then find the smallest whole-number score on the final that pushes your combined total to your target.

1

Add up the totals

Current possible + final total = total marks for the course.

2

Find the target total

Target % × total marks = how many marks you need altogether.

3

Subtract what you already have

What’s left is what the final must cover. We round up — 89.2 becomes 90.

The maths — exactly as we calculate it
required = ceil( target ÷ 100 × (possible + final) − obtained )

Example: 72/80 with a 100-mark final, aiming for 90%.
0.90 × 180 − 72 = 90 → you need 90 / 100.

Show all edge cases we handle

Already enough: if you’d hit the target even with 0, we tell you so and show 0 / total.

Impossible: if even a perfect final can’t reach the target, we show your maximum possible overall.

Fractional: 89.2 → 90, because you need the smallest whole mark that meets the target. Exact 90 stays 90.

Weighted finals: If your syllabus weights the final as “40% of grade” rather than points, this model isn’t exact — we flag it.

How to get the most from the calculator

What numbers do I enter?

Marks you’ve earned — everything so far (e.g. 72). Possible so far — what those were out of (e.g. 80). Final total — what the final is out of (e.g. 100). Target — the overall % you want (e.g. 90).

Same units throughout — if your course is out of 500, enter 360/400 etc.

Why does it round up?

You can’t earn 89.2 on most exams. If the maths says 89.2, 89 won’t get you there — you need 90. If it’s exactly 90, it stays 90. We use a tiny epsilon (1e-9) so 90.0000000001 doesn’t become 91 by mistake.

What if my target is impossible?

We don’t leave you at “Sorry”. We show your maximum possible overall (with a perfect final), your gap in percentage points, and clickable lower targets that are achievable.

Does this work for a 40%-weighted final?

No — not exactly. This calculator uses total points. If your syllabus says “final = 40% of grade regardless of points”, the maths differs. We flag this next to the calculator and explain it in the FAQ.

Quick check: If (current possible + final total) equals your course total, you’re fine. If not, ask your instructor or use a weighted calculator.
